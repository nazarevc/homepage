Thesis

Why agent-first, why now.

The AI-native stack is being built from the ground up. We invest at all three of its layers: below the model, the models themselves, and above the model.

01 · Below the model

Compute and infrastructure

GPU clouds, inference, training data and verification. The next hyperscalers start here.

02 · Models

Open ecosystems

Open weights, sovereign AI and permissionless research. The alternative to walled-garden AI.

03 · Above the model

Agent primitives

Memory, identity, evaluation and security, and the applications built on them. The layer that turns models into autonomous workers.

Founder and General Partner

Dr. Steven Waterhouse

His Cambridge PhD worked on Bayesian mixtures of experts, the architecture inside many of today's frontier models. Since then he has spent 30 years finding the systems layer before the application layer is obvious.

He co-founded RPX Corporation and took it public. At Pantera Capital he invested in Circle's Series A. He co-founded Orchid, a privacy network. Nazaré is his first independent fund.
